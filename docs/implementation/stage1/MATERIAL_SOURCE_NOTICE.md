The published NbN DOS ordinates in `material_nbn_audit.csv` and the material
diagnostic figures are derived from the public data accompanying Simon et al.
The original third-party table remains in the external material data directory.
The two other columns of that CSV identify the original axis and our cumulative
integral, respectively. No correction to the source DOS has been applied.
