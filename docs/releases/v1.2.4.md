## Preparation at reflecting transverse edges

One photon deposits retained energy $E_r$ once, with $0<E_r\le hc/\lambda$.
The new inputs are `--impact-y-nm` and `--deposited-energy-eV`. When explicit
energy is supplied, its retained fraction is $E_r/(hc/\lambda)$ and is recorded
consistently in the identity and source receipt. The initial gap stays at the
current-carrying DC reference; common FD/BE populations are inverted against
the same native total internal-energy functional and its positive-capacity guards.

For $u=y+w/2$, $u_0=y_0+w/2$ and $g_\sigma(z)=\exp[-z^2/(2\sigma^2)]$,

$$K_N(y,y_0)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}
 [g_\sigma(u-u_0+2kw)+g_\sigma(u+u_0+2kw)].$$

$$s_i=g_\sigma(x_i-x_0)K_N(y_i,y_0),\qquad
 E_i=E_r\frac{V_i s_i}{\sum_j V_j s_j}.$$

The longitudinal-cut source weights remain zero. Both transverse boundaries
are reflected, rather than adding only the nearest image. The truncated image
series covers at least nine Gaussian widths; existing nodal dual-volume
quadrature gives exact discrete total-energy normalization. At $y_0=w/2$,
the primary and nearest image coincide: the interior half-Gaussian is doubled,
without doubling total photon energy. At a nonzero distance from the edge,
the image is outside the strip and contributes only its interior tail.

This is a Neumann postcascade PREPARATION hypothesis, not an explicit cascade,
measurement of sidewall reflectivity, ballistic phonon calculation, second
photon or time-dependent reinjection. Substrate phonon escape remains20ps.
Reference for the reflecting heat-kernel construction and conservation:
[Farrington and Gittins (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00020-023-02749-0).

## Reuse of the four completed Rs608 bases

The four baseline cases are immutable1.2.3 latency-only trajectories.
Authentication requires exact native source pins, physical/numerical identities,
checkpoint/DC hashes and final saved distributions/gap/circuit. Only scheduling
fields and the requested observation continuation may change. A separate
scratch directory contains an independent/reflink copy, original manifest and
checkpoint, hashes, and an explicit provenance receipt. No photon is redeposited.

The1D shadow starts at the last saved2D snapshot at/before the trigger,
at most0.1ps earlier, and evolves independently against exact simultaneous
saved2D states through trigger+2ps. The2D equations are never reintegrated.
Existing projection, population/energy, amplitude/current, voltage, winding,
domain and circuit gates admit or reject the handoff without relaxed tolerances.
Official1D starts from the final conservatively projected2D state and evolved
native tails; its1000nm domain and frozen DC inductance partition are preserved.

All four real baselines passed the replay and handoff, with22 overlap checks
each, and advanced0.02ps into official1D. These bounded checks took100-142s
per case, including authentication/setup/replay and brief1D evolution. Their
validated checkpoints seed the supplied recovery campaign, so this work is reused.
The eight-hour native1D wall budget is PER CASE/PER SESSION; reaching it saves
a resumable checkpoint. The15ns physical target and existing domain/electrical/
recovery/cold-admission guards remain. Passing the handoff is not proof of15ns
completion, full reset or experimental retrigger readiness.

## Concurrent campaigns and progress

Each command starts four simultaneous cases with3Numba threads per case,
BLAS/OpenMP1, six physical worker cores with declared SMT sharing between pairs,
and one separate coordinator core. Two campaigns fit28/32 logical CPUs while
reserving two COMPLETE physical cores. At admission, topology/quotas/RAM and
known active workers are rechecked. Atomic live-PID/start-time leases prevent
two screens from racing for the same CPUs; stale leases expire safely, and
existing workers are never repinned or interrupted. Per-campaign locks prevent
duplicate launches. The native-source argv marker preserves visibility to the
unchanged legacy scanner. Other active jobs can require waiting for capacity.

Each screen redraws an English four-row board: phase,time,Vout,step,accepted/
rejected steps, rolling speed,ETA,1Dsession budget and CPU/RAM where width allows.
Replay time is distinct from saved final2D time. Full logs remain in scratch.
Ctrl-C/SIGTERM targets only that campaign's children; `--resume` continues own
checkpoint pauses. Diagnostically failed handoffs/states are preserved, not retried
as if admitted. Long production runs are prepared, never started by this release task.
