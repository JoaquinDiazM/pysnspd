# pysnspd 1.2.5 - authenticated electrical 0D continuation

Released 2026-10-29. This release adds a distinct electrical-only `0d` phase
after native `1d`. It does not change the 2D/1D evolution kernel, thermal
equations, numerical tolerances, original data or historical producer hashes.
The native producers 13/18/19 retain their historical identities intentionally.
Use `pipelines/25_v125_electrical_phase.py` for one phase handoff and
`pipelines/26_v125_recovery_pair.py` for the two Rs608 low-bias continuations.

## Equations and energy scope

Restore the resolved 1D strip's frozen DC differential inductance to the
external inductance. For the 15.5 uA references,

$$L_f=1.428715525273054\,\mathrm{nH},\qquad
 L_d=L_{\mathrm{ext,1D}}+L_f=103.14357762636527\,\mathrm{nH}.$$

All 96 nH of added exterior inductance remain. The measured state at the actual
native checkpoint is inherited exactly; there is no rewind to 12 ns, photon
reinjection or imposed DC reset. Global time remains the original postcascade
2D clock. The three actual circuit states satisfy

$$\dot I_b={V_b-R_bI_b-v_c-R_L(I_b-I_s)\over L_b},\quad
\dot I_s={v_c+R_L(I_b-I_s)\over L_d},\quad
\dot v_c={I_b-I_s\over C}.$$

$$V_{\mathrm{out}}=R_L(I_b-I_s),\qquad V_f=L_f\dot I_s.$$

The film terminal voltage is retained; it is not forced to zero. Inductance is
constant, inferred once from the authenticated DC partition, with no latency or
tail fit. Native populations, temperature and gap stop evolving at handoff.
No artificial cold fields are written. This explicitly replaces the subsequent
spatial thermal evolution with an electrical surrogate, not full cold recovery.

The 0D electrical energy is

$$E_c=\tfrac12 L_bI_b^2+\tfrac12 L_dI_s^2+\tfrac12 Cv_c^2,\qquad
\dot E_c=V_bI_b-R_bI_b^2-R_L(I_b-I_s)^2.$$

A matrix exponential evolves circuit deviations from DC. An independent
polynomial lift integrates net source-minus-Joule work, analytically cancelling
the DC source/resistor baseline before floating-point evaluation. The native
energy/work ledger and defect are inherited unchanged at the interface. After
it, an explicitly frozen unresolved-energy offset plus the lumped electrical
energy defines the reduced ledger; this is not a continued native thermal
conservation claim. A separate phase defect is recorded and checked against the
unchanged native local-energy tolerance. Tiny final current deviations are
retained separately to avoid loss of precision near DC.

## Admission monitor

The earliest declared scope is 12 ns; time alone does not admit a case. Defaults
require a complete one-nanosecond accepted native overlap ending at the current
checkpoint, positive global currents, clear hot-segment/domain monitors, native
energy/cut bounds and current source integrity. Full-domain gap amplitudes must
stay within 3% of the current-matched imposed cold boundary profile; saved gap
fields must cover the overlap with no more than 10 ps gaps. These are explicit
engineering reduction bounds, not measured material uncertainties.

An independent three-state circuit is initialized at the beginning of that
window and compared at every saved accepted native time. Maximum Vout, capacitor
voltage and noninductive film-voltage residual errors must each be <=1 uV;
current overlap error must be <=0.5% of initial bias. The film normal-current
scale fraction |Vfilm|/(Rnormal |Is|) must be <=0.001. The existing native
physical/numerical failure states cannot be cleared by this path. Ineligible
cases remain native and require further native evolution; the electrical entry
does not silently launch an expensive 1D fallback.

Bath temperature is deliberately not an electrical admission condition. Record
the last full native checkpoint, populations, temperatures and pending thermal
audit instead of claiming full reset or retrigger readiness.

## Compatibility and archives

Supported sources are intact paused/completed native **1D** checkpoints produced
by 1.2.3 or 1.2.4. Native 2D-only latency data still require their authenticated
1D continuation first. Authentication requires the exact supported source
catalog, file hashes, matching checkpoint/manifest physical identities and counts,
exact final saved gap/p/n/circuit, and native-only regime IDs. Older failures or
already reconstructed cold trajectories are refused.

A separate scratch output contains:

- `native_manifest.json`, `native_checkpoint.npz`, and `dc1d.npz`: unchanged
  authenticated native evidence, with the original p/n/gap arrays retained.
- `data.h5`: read-only external links to committed native 2d/shadow1d/1d history,
  plus a local `0d/trace` group. Frozen prefix counts are authoritative.
- `phase_plan.json`, `manifest.json`, `checkpoint_latest.npz`: electrical
  policy/provenance, independent new source hashes, actual absolute time,
  precise deviations, integrated work and committed trace counts.

Original archives are never copied or rewritten. Keep the linked native source
directories available and immutable; their checksum/signature is checked again
on resume and by the 0D report reader. Native producer source pins are separate
from new-phase source pins; an old checkpoint is never relabeled as newly evolved
native physics. `--resume` retains completed outputs, resumes an own clean pause
and refuses corrupted/diagnostic states. SIGINT/SIGTERM save the new phase only.

Use `plot_pipelines/10_v125_phase_history.py` to inspect all three phases; legacy
07/08 native reports remain untouched. The 0D group has no fake Te/gap/p/n fields.

## Resources, validation and limitation

The single-case execution entry requires an admitted `--cpu-list`; the pair
coordinator allocates it automatically.

The pair coordinator shares the existing atomic live-PID CPU lease registry.
Each case uses one numerical thread and a distinct physical worker core; a
third core is reserved for coordination. At least two whole physical cores stay
free, with the 90% CPU/RAM ceiling rechecked. Native source campaign locks are
held for reading and active producers are refused, never stopped.

Both real checkpoints passed the final 1 ns overlap: 8518/9195 comparisons,
maximum Vout errors 0.017028/0.019743 uV, and maximum full-domain gap departure
0.532143/0.573791%. Separate bounded validation continuations reached 100 ns in
about five seconds of phase execution per case, with native manifest/checkpoint/DC
hashes unchanged. Reduced energy-defect fractions remained below 1.1e-16.
This is measured 0D cost, not a prediction of native 1D runtime.

The unresolved thermal-recovery behavior is archived in
[`v125_thermal_recovery_pending.md`](../audits/v125_thermal_recovery_pending.md).
No physical explanation or repair is asserted by this release. The new phase
certifies its electrical reduction criteria, not experimental jitter/PCR,
full reset, late quasiparticles, or retriggering. See the machine-readable
`v1.2.5_checks.json` for the completed validation record.
