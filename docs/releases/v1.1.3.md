# pysnspd 1.1.3: DC-matched finite-depairing thermal correction

The 1.1.2 anchors stalled near3.00094 ps (775 nm) and4.50652 ps (1550 nm). Version1.1.3 replaces their strong-gradient quadratic thermal extrapolation, preserves the calibrated DC, circuit and postcascade photon source, and accelerates the complete coupled midpoint. This effective revision does not establish quantitative latency or microscopic Usadel kinetics.

## Physical diagnosis and replacement

At frozen gap/gradient, the old G=kappa(rho,Te)L closure had intervals with Ceff<0: captured-state scans reached approximately−0.3633 Cfd and−0.2198 Cfd. The stalled states had Ceff/Cfd of order1e−5 and strong phase gradients. This is a constitutive limitation; a frozen-field scan does not prove how every complete trajectory crosses its domain boundary.

The new closure is
$$
G_i=\kappa(\rho_i,T_b)L_i+
u_i^*[\varphi(\rho_i,T_i/T_c,Q_i)-\varphi(\rho_i,T_b/T_c,Q_i)],\quad
Q_i=L_i/(u_i^*\rho_i).
$$
Only the finite-depairing thermal correction is resummed. The bath/DC functional matches exactly and the weak-gradient limit recovers Te-dependent quadratic rigidity. Rebuilding the actual closure retains D=1.4103757147 cm²/s and the25.8667 µA width-scaled depairing target within roundoff.

The matching term
$$
H_i^{\rm match}=\kappa(\rho_i,T_b)L_i-u_i^*\varphi(\rho_i,T_b/T_c,Q_i)
$$
is temperature independent. At fixed rho,L, G→Hmatch as Te→∞: nonzero elastic reference remains. It is an explicit effective hypothesis, not measured hot-state elasticity or a result attributed to Clem. It introduces no free latency parameter.

Early saved AA states quantify its role: at the hottest node, Hmatch/G is70.55% for775 nm at4.54 ps and46.17% for1550 nm at4.16 ps; residual G_L fractions are82.91% and66.78%. Whole-domain Hmatch/G is9.83% and6.34%. These are local/time-specific diagnostics, not universal percentages or final-8-ps results.

Native collision/transport spectra retain local q=0 BCS. Qeff combines amplitude and phase gradients as an effective graph law. No current-dependent depairing DOS, initial ultrafast cascade or lateral phonon diffusion is evolved. These restrictions can affect hotbelt growth and wavelength-dependent latency; energy conservation alone does not validate experimental rates.

## Numerical changes

Full coupled Anderson accelerates gap,p,n,circuit; safeguarded temperature inversion replaces fixed-count bisection. Analytic finite-depairing partials include controlled Matsubara tails, normal limits, exact Q=0 identities and explicit parallel-worker failure detection. Acceptance remains on the original physical residual and independent energy guard, with the original step-scaled tolerance. No capacity/stiffness floor, population clipping or residual-derived heat is added.
