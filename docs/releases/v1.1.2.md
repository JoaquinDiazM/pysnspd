The 1.1.1 anchors stalled near3.02 ps(775 nm) and4.51 ps(1550 nm). A directly reproduced accepted step had h=1.9073486328125e-8 ps, nonzero population RHS1048.6757/ps, zero nonlinear iterations and exactly zero change in gap, electron/phonon populations and circuit. Version1.1.2 requires an actual coupled correction, scales the thermal nonlinear budget with step size, checks the full thermal response inside convergence and backtracks internal corrections to keep positive capacity. It preserves the physical equations, Te stiffness, calibrated diffusion, Pauli heat, circuit and independent energy guard. ## Ordered investigation

1. **Continuous physics:** the captured states still had positive capacity. Minimum Ceff/Cfd was3.0322e-4 for775 nm and5.4133e-7 for1550 nm. Directional derivatives along the actual continuous RHS increased Ceff, approximately7.465 and5477.55 times Cfd per ps. Additionally, the complete Te-induced population-to-gap check occurred only after the inherited solver returned; that failure caused timestep rejection rather than further nonlinear iterations. Internal positivity damping covered Pauli/Bose states, not the thermal-capacity domain.
3. **Implementation:** require iteration>0 at acceptance, include the complete thermal response in every convergence norm, and add internal coupled capacity-domain backtracking. Retain the analytic population-direction calculation without evaluating an unsupported perturbed population.

## Error budget

With full midpoint residual g_n=h_n F(X_mid)-deltaX_n, the old per-step absolute budget could accumulate as N*epsilon0 independently of simulated time. The thermal solver now uses

$$
\epsilon_{\rm solve}(h)=\epsilon_0\min\left(1,\frac{h}{h_{\rm ref}}\right),
\qquad \epsilon_0=2\times10^{-7},\quad h_{\rm ref}=0.02\,{\rm ps}.
$$

Thus the normalized nonlinear defect sum is bounded by epsilon0*T/h_ref for steps below the reference cap, rather than by an unbounded count of tiny accepted predictors. This controls algebraic solve error, not temporal discretization, spatial convergence or experimental validity. The new margin is stricter for small steps. At least one coupled update is mandatory; exact equilibrium may still remain stationary.

Backtracking changes only the internal path to the same midpoint root. Accepted physical equations, unsigned kinetic-energy residuals, complete gap guard, Pauli/Bose support, positive global currents and independent energy transaction remain required. No residual-derived heat, occupation clipping or capacity floor is introduced.

## Declared numerical-resolution stop

The new candidate option minimum_adaptive_step_ps defaults to1e-6 ps. If a rejected step would demand a smaller adaptive step, preserve the last accepted checkpoint and report NUMERICAL_RESOLUTION_LIMIT. This is an explicit computational admissibility limit, not a minimum timestep substituted into the equations, a detected photon, or temporal censoring at100 ps. A future change of this limit requires a justified diagnostic decision; do not restart blindly.

An end-to-end test deliberately limits the nonlinear solver to one iteration and forces this stop at t=0. It verifies a finite full checkpoint, unchanged accepted clock and no detection. It is a transaction test, not photon admission.
